Phase 4 • HR, Payroll & Bahrain VAT

Staff Records, Bank Payroll & 10% Tax

Automate administrative paperwork so company management can focus on sales growth.

🪪 LMRA & Visa Expiry Alerts
Logs staff CPR, driver licenses, and work permits with automated 60-day renewal warnings to prevent government fines.
🏦 WPS Salary Bank File (SIF)
Calculates basic salaries, commissions, and GOSI deductions—then exports the official WPS file ready for NBB, BBK, Ila, or AUB.
🧾 Automated 10% NBR VAT Invoices
Generates compliant Bahrain tax invoices for every wholesale delivery, showing DAS CR and VAT numbers automatically.
📊 Quarterly Tax Filing Export
Summarizes Input VAT on stock purchases vs. Output VAT on drink sales so filing with the National Bureau for Revenue takes 5 minutes.

Why this matters for DAS Bahrain
Guarantees 100% legal compliance with Bahrain LMRA, Ministry of Labour WPS wages protection, and NBR tax regulations without needing full-time outside consultants.
