1. Executive Overview & Strategic Intent

Dar Al Baba Salam (DAS Bahrain) has deployed an enterprise-grade digital portal representing its FMCG portfolio and core beverage distribution lines across Bahrain—led by the licensed distribution of Alsi Cola. To maximize profitability, streamline van sales operations, and eliminate costly third-party software subscriptions (such as SAP or monolithic ERPs), this initiative transforms the public-facing storefront into an end-to-end Distribution Management System (DMS).

3. Target Architecture: One Unified Relational Core

Rather than maintaining disconnected software systems, DAS Bahrain will scale a single PostgreSQL relational database protected by enterprise Row-Level Security (RLS). This guarantees complete data isolation between external shoppers, verified B2B grocers, field drivers, and internal management.

CENTRAL POSTGRESQL DATABASE & ROW-LEVEL SECURITY (RLS)

Public store, authenticated login, saved Bahrain delivery pins, live delivery tracker.
B2B Trade Grocers
Commercial Registration (CR) verification, tier-discounted carton rates, instant credit reorders.

Driver route manifests (`/driver`), warehouse load-out count, on-site e-delivery notes.

Daily cash settlements, driver targets, LMRA/GOSI compliance, WPS payroll, 10% NBR VAT.

4. Four-Phase Modular Implementation Roadmap
Phase 1: Customer Auth & Order Tracking
Month 1 (30 Days)
  • Customer login via Bahrain Mobile OTP or secure Email credentials.
  • Saved delivery address book (Governorate, Block, Road, Building).
  • Live Order Pipeline: `Received` ➔ `Confirmed` ➔ `Packed at Hidd` ➔ `Out for Delivery` ➔ `Delivered`.
  • Customer purchase history and one-click re-ordering.
Phase 2: B2B Merchant Hub & Route Manifests
Month 2 (35 Days)
  • B2B Cold Store & Baqala registration verified via Bahrain CR.
  • Dynamic wholesale discount pricing tiers hidden from general retail.
  • Mobile Driver Route Module (`/driver`) with assigned daily route stops.
  • Instant electronic proof-of-delivery (e-POD) with signature capture.
Phase 3: Driver Performance & Cash Audit
Month 3 (30 Days)
  • Daily Carton Targets vs. Actuals: Track driver targets for Alsi Cola and partner FMCG brands.
  • Store visit compliance monitoring (visited vs. bypassed).
  • End-of-Shift Reconciliation: Load-out inventory vs. physical returns vs. cash & BenefitPay receipts.
  • Automated commission ledger tied to verified driver delivery logs.
Phase 4: HR, WPS Payroll & Bahrain 10% VAT
Month 4 (35 Days)
  • Employee CPR registry, visa, and LMRA work permit renewal alerts.
  • Salary calculation with base pay, overtime, commissions, and GOSI deductions.
  • WPS Export: SIF salary transfer file generation for Bahrain banks (NBB, BBK, AUB, Ila).
  • Automated 10% NBR VAT audit ledger with Input/Output quarterly reporting.
